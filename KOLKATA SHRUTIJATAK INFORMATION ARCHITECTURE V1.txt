11. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE RULES

	•	Admissions reachable within 1 click.
	•	No page deeper than 3 clicks.
	•	Homepage previews only.
	•	Program details separated from listing.
	•	Faculty authority isolated into profiles.
	•	Events are always current or archived.
	•	Gallery is visual-first.
	•	Contact always accessible.
	•	Apply CTA always visible.
	•	Mobile nav simplified.
	•	Internal links reinforce SEO clusters.
	•	Journal supports authority growth.
	•	No dead-end pages.
	•	Every page has a CTA.
	•	Conversion never hidden.

SYSTEM SUMMARY
This IA creates:
	•	clear discovery
	•	high trust
	•	emotional resonance
	•	SEO scalability
	•	conversion funnel
without bloating the homepage into a digital traffic jam.
