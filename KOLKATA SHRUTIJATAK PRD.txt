SECTION 04
Core Website Goals
The website must achieve five experiential goals. These are not features — they are outcomes that the design, content, and UX must collectively produce.
Goal 1 — Instant Identity (0–3 Seconds)
— Visitor understands what Kolkata Shrutijatak is within three seconds of landing.
— Hero section communicates: institution name, cultural category, what is offered, and why it matters.
— No ambiguity. No hunting. No reading required to grasp the core identity.

Goal 2 — Perceived Premiumness
— The site must feel like a serious institution, not a hobby school or community centre.
— Faculty credentials, student outcomes, real events, and media presence must validate quality.
— Every visual and typographic choice must reinforce institutional seriousness.

Goal 3 — Crystal-Clear Offering
— Visitor must immediately understand: which programs exist, who they are for, how to join.
— Zero confusion about disciplines, levels, eligibility, or enrollment process.
— Programs section must read like a premium product catalogue, not a course list.

Goal 4 — Emotional Desire
— Website must create the feeling: 'I want to be part of this institution.'
— Achieved through beautiful visuals, student life content, performance media, cultural depth.
— Memorable 'shock moments' — 2–3 high-impact sections that stop the scroll.

Goal 5 — Zero-Friction Conversion
— At any point in the journey, user can apply, contact, or ask questions in one action.
— No dead ends. No confusion. Apply CTA always within reach.
— Admissions section feels ceremonious, not bureaucratic.

SECTION 06
Homepage Sections
The homepage is the institution's digital front door and must deliver a complete emotional and informational journey. Sections must follow the rhythm: Impact → Information → Emotion → Proof → Conversion.
Section 6.1 — Hero (Shock Moment 1)
Purpose: Immediate identity + cinematic first impression
— Full-screen immersive layout — 100vh minimum
— Dominant cultural visual: instrument, performer silhouette, or cultural artifact composition
— Large refined institution name: Kolkata Shrutijatak (English + Bengali)
— Short positioning statement beneath: one line maximum
— Two primary CTAs: Apply Now (primary, crimson), Explore Programs (secondary, outlined)
— Subtle ambient motion — slow reveal, layered depth, not kinetic chaos
— Sticky transparent nav that becomes solid on scroll
Emotional target: cinematic arrival. Visitor feels they have entered something significant.
Section 6.2 — Institutional Introduction
Purpose: Establish legitimacy and philosophy
— Brief editorial paragraph about the institution — origin, mission, essence
— One strong philosophical statement set in large type
— Supporting imagery: archival or stage photography
— Link to full About page
Section 6.3 — Disciplines / Programs Overview
Purpose: Communicate the core offering
— Grid or card system presenting all disciplines
— Each card: discipline name, one-line description, and a visual anchor
— Hover interaction: card elevation or image reveal
— CTA per card: Explore [Discipline]
— Section headline: something declarative, not generic
Section 6.4 — Cultural Immersion Moment (Shock Moment 2)
Purpose: Emotional depth — the soul of the institution
— Full-screen or near full-screen visual section
— Could be: performance still, stage photograph, cultural artifact composition
— Overlaid with a powerful institutional statement — not marketing copy, but cultural declaration
— This is the scroll-stopper. The section visitors remember.
— No CTA required here — pure emotional impact only
Section 6.5 — Faculty Highlight
Purpose: Trust and credibility
— 3–4 featured faculty profiles in portrait-led layout
— Name, specialisation, key credential
— Link to full faculty page
— Tone: serious and authoritative, not warm-and-friendly
Section 6.6 — Upcoming Events
Purpose: Demonstrate institutional activity and current relevance
— 3 upcoming events in a visual list or card format
— Each entry: event name, date, type (performance / workshop / audition)
— CTA: View All Events
— Empty state fallback: 'Stay tuned for upcoming programmes' — never show blank section
Section 6.7 — Student Achievements / Testimonials
Purpose: Social proof and aspiration
— 2–3 student success stories or testimonials
— Formats: quote with name and discipline, or short outcome statement
— Could include competition wins, certifications, career outcomes
— Photographic portraits preferred — real faces, not stock imagery
Section 6.8 — Gallery Preview
Purpose: Visual proof and emotional atmosphere
— 6–9 curated images in a masonry or editorial grid
— Mix of: performances, classes, campus, instruments, cultural moments
— CTA: Explore Full Gallery
— High production standard — no casual phone photos in this preview section
Section 6.9 — Admissions Invitation (Shock Moment 3)
Purpose: Final conversion moment — ceremonious, not bureaucratic
— Full-width section with strong contrast: dark background, crimson or gold accents
— Headline: declarative, aspirational — not 'Apply Here'
— Short enrollment invitation copy: 2–3 lines maximum
— Primary CTA: Begin Your Application
— Secondary: Download Prospectus / Contact Admissions
— This section should feel like an invitation to join something significant

01. Visual Language & "Shock" Definitions
The primary failure point of previous versions was the ambiguity of "futurism." These definitions now enforce a museum-quality aesthetic.
1.1 Visual System Rules
•	Negative Space: 85% of the layout must use "White/Bone" or "Deep Charcoal" negative space to ensure an editorial, museum feel.
•	Color Palette: * Primary: Crimson (Accent only for CTAs/links).
•	Base: Charcoal Typography on Bone/Off-white backgrounds.
•	Accents: Warm Gold for micro-details (borders, icons).
•	Typography: Editorial-grade Serif for Bengali/Headings; Ultra-clean Sans-serif for body text.
1.2 Defining the "Shock Moments"
"Shock" does not mean glitch, neon, or complex animation. It means Impact through Scale and Quality.
	1.	Shock Moment 1 (Hero): A cinematic, high-resolution composition of a single cultural object (e.g., an Esraj or a Sitar) with layered depth (parallax) and sharp, elegant typography.
	2.	Shock Moment 2 (Immersion): A full-screen, silent, slow-motion video or high-grain archival photograph of a performance, overlaid with a philosophical statement.
	3.	Shock Moment 3 (Admissions): A high-contrast transition (e.g., flipping from light mode to deep charcoal/gold) to signal the "Invitation to Join."
02. Institutional Structure & Sitemap
A scalable architecture designed for trust and SEO.
2.1 Core Navigation (Phase 1)
•	Home: The Identity Hub.
•	About: The Founding Story (Piu Biswas Das).
•	Programs: The "Product Catalog" of arts (Music, Dance, Drama).
•	Faculty: Credibility through expert profiles.
•	Admissions: The Ceremonious Inquiry Engine.
•	Contact: Global and local accessibility.
03. Mandatory Asset Manifest
No AI-generated "ghost" imagery. Every asset must be intentional.

04. Technical & Business Constraints
Optimized for a zero-cost, high-performance stack.
•	Performance: < 2s load time on 4G mobile; "All Green" Core Web Vitals.
•	SEO Moat: Schema.org (EducationalOrganization) and Bengali Unicode indexing.
•	Zero-Cost CMS (MVP): Utilize Markdown/Content Collections or local JSON files via GitHub-based workflow instead of expensive SaaS CMS.
•	Conversion: Sticky "Apply Now" (Crimson) must be within thumb-reach on 60%+ of sessions (mobile).

To prevent the "code-first" trap, the following documents must be generated before a single line of CSS is written:

	3.	Homepage Wireframe v1: Low-fidelity layout focusing on the "Impact → Information → Emotion" rhythm.
	4.	Motion Language v1: Defining the "Slow Reveal" and "Depth Layering" logic (No kinetic chaos).
