08. MOTION LANGUAGE V1

Motion Principles
	•	Slow
	•	Elegant
	•	Controlled
	•	Expensive-feeling
No:
	•	glitch
	•	bounce
	•	spinning
	•	chaos

Hero Reveal
Sequence:
	•	Fade in image/object
	•	Text rise
	•	Slight parallax activation
Duration: 1.8–2.5s

Scroll Reveal
Fade + translate Y 20–40px
Duration: 0.8–1.2s

Hover Motion
Cards:
	•	lift 4–8px
Images:
	•	scale 1.03–1.05
Buttons:
	•	color transition only

Parallax Rules
Slow only.
Max 8–12%.

Section Transition
Opacity shift.
Soft background transition.

Shock Moments
Exactly 3.
Shock 1
Hero arrival
Shock 2
Cultural immersion fullscreen
Shock 3
Admissions contrast inversion

11. DESIGN PRINCIPLES SUMMARY
Non-negotiable rules:
	•	White/Bone negative space dominates.
	•	Crimson is sacred; use sparingly.
	•	Maximum 3 shock moments only.
	•	Motion must feel expensive, never busy.
	•	Every page must have one clear CTA.
	•	Typography carries authority.
	•	Bengali identity must feel integrated, not decorative.
	•	Images must feel curated, never random.
	•	Mobile is primary.
	•	No cyber aesthetics.
	•	No OS simulation.
	•	No glitch effects.
	•	Every section must breathe.
	•	Premium > flashy.
	•	Conversion clarity beats visual experimentation.

SYSTEM SUMMARY
Visual Identity: A premium Bengali cultural institution from the near future.
Feels like: Apple Store × museum editorial website × cultural monument.
Not: Startup dashboard, cyberpunk concept art, or experimental chaos.
